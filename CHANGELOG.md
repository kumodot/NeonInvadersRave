# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-11)

First public release.

- Space Invaders remix with vector glow visuals and a procedural rave soundtrack (Web Audio, no samples).
- Heat system: kills unlock 8 music layers (kick, hats, clap, bass, acid, lead, stabs, trip). Tempo, enemy fire and visuals scale with the layer.
- Shots snap to the beat grid. PERFECT hits build a GROOVE multiplier (up to x8).
- Break and drop every 8 bars from the acid layer up. One bar of piercing BEAM shots after the drop.
- Galaga style fighters peel off the formation and dive at the shields.
- Rhythm bomb (X): invaders in the blast radius stop shooting and dance for 4 bars.
- Parallax starfield that accelerates with the music, beat driven UI metronomes.
- Keyboard, gamepad (Steam Deck friendly) and touch controls. Fullscreen with F.
- High score saved in the browser (localStorage).
