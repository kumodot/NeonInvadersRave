## Neon Invaders Rave v1.0.0

First public release.

- **Soundtrack as difficulty**: kills build heat, heat unlocks music layers from a bare kick to a full trip. Tempo goes from 140 to 154 BPM.
- **Beat quantized shots**: every shot lands on the grid. Tap on the beat for *PERFECT* and a *GROOVE* multiplier.
- **Break and drop**: invaders freeze for a bar, then the drop clears the screen and your shots pierce.
- **Fighters**: Galaga style dives from the formation, worth x3.
- **Rhythm bomb** (*X*): turns invaders into dancers for 4 bars, worth x2.
- **Controls**: keyboard, gamepad (Steam Deck) and touch.

Run: open `NeonInvadersRave_v1.0.0.html`, or online at https://kumodot.github.io/NeonInvadersRave/
